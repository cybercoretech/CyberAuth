01 / AUTHENTICATOR, FOCUSED

Less friction.
Clearer control.

CyberAuth follows the practical strengths of modern authenticator apps: fast code access, standards compatibility, and privacy that does not depend on an account in the cloud.

01 / STANDARD◷

Compatible TOTP

Generate RFC 6238 time-based codes with SHA-1, SHA-256, or SHA-512 parameters from an authenticator setup link.

02 / PRIVATE⌑

Local by design

Codes are calculated on the phone. The app has no Internet permission, analytics, sign-in, or remote account database.

03 / PROTECTED⬡

Encrypted at rest

Account data is AES-256-GCM encrypted with a device Keystore key. Portable backups use a separate passphrase and authenticated encryption.

04 / PRACTICAL⧉

Scan or enter

Scan setup QR codes on-device, enter a Base32 key, or paste an otpauth:// link. Camera access is requested only when scanning.

02 / KNOW THE BOUNDARIES

Private storage
with portable recovery.

CyberAuth is early development software. Device-local encryption protects stored entries at rest. You can also export a passphrase-encrypted backup and restore it on another device; CyberAuth cannot recover a forgotten backup passphrase.

PROTECTS TODAY

Encrypted local account storage, on-device QR scanning, passphrase-encrypted backups, no Internet permission, and no automatic Android cloud backup.

NOT HERE YET

Biometric app lock, cloud sync, SMS assistance, and independent security audit.

YOUR SAFETY NET

Keep the backup passphrase separately from the backup file, test restore, retain service recovery codes, and maintain another sign-in method.
