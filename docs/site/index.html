01 / AUTHENTICATOR, FOCUSED

Less friction.
Clearer control.

CyberAuth follows the practical strengths of modern authenticator apps: fast code access, standards compatibility, and privacy that does not depend on an account in the cloud.

01 / STANDARD◷

Compatible TOTP

Generate RFC 6238 time-based codes with SHA-1, SHA-256, or SHA-512 parameters from an authenticator setup link.

02 / PRIVATE⌑

Local by design

Codes are calculated on the phone. The first build has no network permission, analytics, sign-in, or remote account database.

03 / PROTECTED⬡

Encrypted at rest

Account data is AES-256-GCM encrypted with a device Keystore key. The current build is device-local and has no recovery export yet.

04 / PRACTICAL⧉

Quick to use

See a live expiry timer, copy a code with one tap, and add accounts with a setup key or an otpauth:// link.

02 / KNOW THE BOUNDARIES

Private storage
is not a backup.

CyberAuth is early development software. Device-local encryption protects stored entries at rest, but the current milestone cannot restore your accounts after app data is cleared, the app is uninstalled, or the device key is lost.

PROTECTS TODAY

Encrypted local account storage, a Keystore-held key, no network permission, and no automatic Android cloud backup.

NOT HERE YET

Biometric app lock, QR camera import, portable encrypted recovery, sync, SMS assistance, and independent security audit.

YOUR SAFETY NET

Keep the service’s recovery codes separately and maintain another sign-in method. Do not make this early build your only path in.
